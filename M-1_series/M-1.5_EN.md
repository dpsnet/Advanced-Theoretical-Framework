### 6.1 Spectral Triple Framework on Fractal Manifolds

A spectral triple $(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{H}, \mathcal{D})$ on a fractal manifold consists of:
1. An involutive algebra $\mathcal{A}$ of functions on the fractal manifold (observable algebra);
2. A Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}$ carrying a representation of $\mathcal{A}$;
3. A self-adjoint unbounded Dirac operator $\mathcal{D}$ with compact resolvent.

**Definition 6.1.1 (Fractal Spectral Triple)**: A spectral triple $(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{H}, \mathcal{D})$ on a fractal manifold $X$ with metric $d$ and measure $\mu$ satisfies:
1. **Commutation Relation**: $[\mathcal{D}, a]$ extends to a bounded operator for all $a \in \mathcal{A}$;
2. **Compact Resolvent**: $(\mathcal{D} - \lambda)^{-1}$ is compact for $\lambda \notin \mathrm{spec}(\mathcal{D})$;
3. **Finite Summability**: $\mathrm{Tr}\,|\mathcal{D}|^{-p} < \infty$ for some $p > 0$.

**Theorem 6.1.1**: For a fractal manifold $(X,d,\mu)$ with Ahlfors $Q$-regular measure, there exists a spectral triple $(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{H}, \mathcal{D})$ with spectral dimension $d_s = Q$.

### 6.2 Spectral Dimensions and Fractal Dimensions

**Definition 6.2.1 (Spectral Dimension)**: For a spectral triple $(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{H}, \mathcal{D})$, the spectral dimension is defined as:
$$d_s = \inf\{p > 0 \mid \mathrm{Tr}\,|\mathcal{D}|^{-p} < \infty\}$$

**Definition 6.2.2 (Fractal Dimension)**: For a metric space $(X,d)$, the Hausdorff dimension is:
$$d_H = \inf\{\alpha > 0 \mid \mathcal{H}^\alpha(X) = 0\}$$
where $\mathcal{H}^\alpha$ is the $\alpha$-dimensional Hausdorff measure.

**Theorem 6.2.1**: For a self-similar fractal $K \subset \mathbb{R}^n$ with dimension $d_H$, there exists a spectral triple $(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{H}, \mathcal{D})$ with:
$$d_s = d_H$$

### 6.3 Dirac Operator Realization

On a Riemannian manifold $(M,g)$, the Dirac operator is defined as:
$$\mathcal{D} = \gamma^\mu \nabla_\mu = i\gamma^\mu \partial_\mu$$
where $\gamma^\mu$ are gamma matrices satisfying $\{\gamma^\mu, \gamma^\nu\} = 2g^{\mu\nu}$.

**Definition 6.3.1 (Fractal Dirac Operator)**: On a fractal manifold $(X,d,\mu)$ with spectral dimension $d_s$, the Dirac operator $\mathcal{D}$ is characterized by its spectrum:
$$\mathrm{spec}(\mathcal{D}) = \{\pm\lambda_k\}_{k=1}^\infty, \quad \lambda_k \sim k^{1/d_s}$$

**Theorem 6.3.1**: The fractal Dirac operator $\mathcal{D}$ satisfies the Lichnerowicz formula:
$$\mathcal{D}^2 = \Delta + \frac{1}{4}R$$
where $\Delta$ is the Laplacian and $R$ is the scalar curvature, appropriately generalized to fractal spaces.
